# Scrutium AI

**Scrutium AI** is an AI workspace built by X-ion. Its documentation covers conversational assistance, research workflows, project organization, creation tools, personalization, account settings, and developer configuration.

## Core capabilities

- **Chat:** ask questions, write, brainstorm, and work through multi-step tasks.
- **Research tools:** use supported live-data tools when available in the deployed environment.
- **Projects:** organize related conversations and work.
- **Build mode:** create and iterate on small apps or pages within supported workflows.
- **Library:** find and manage supported files, media, and saved items.
- **Memory and personalization:** manage the context and preferences available to the assistant.
- **Account and safety controls:** configure supported account, privacy, and safety options.
