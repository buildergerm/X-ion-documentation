# Draft

**Draft** is an X-ion product for campaign and content workflows. It helps users work with content and connect external services that support publishing, commerce, collaboration, and analytics.

## Typical workflow

1. Open Draft and sign in through the configured account flow.
2. Start a campaign or open an existing campaign.
3. Select existing content or create the content required for the campaign.
4. Choose the channels or services relevant to the campaign.
5. Connect any required provider account and review its requested permissions.
6. Check the selected content, destination, schedule, and any provider-specific requirements before publishing or submitting changes.
7. Review the campaign status and available reporting after the action completes.

The exact controls and available actions can vary by account, provider, and deployment. Do not assume a post has been published or a campaign has been sent until Draft reports a successful result.

## Integrations

Draft's integration directory has included services across these categories:

- **Social and publishing:** Meta Business, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, and Pinterest.
- **Commerce:** Shopify.
- **Collaboration:** Slack.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics 4.

The integrations shown in a particular Draft environment may differ. Each provider can require its own account, administrator approval, OAuth authorization, permissions, API configuration, or product-side enablement.

[Read the integration guide](/docs/products/draft-integrations)

## Connection safety

- Connect only accounts you are authorized to manage.
- Review requested permissions before approving access.
- Never paste access tokens, client secrets, or API keys into documentation, campaign content, or support messages.
- If a connection fails, record the provider, visible error, and time of failure without exposing credentials, then consult the provider's permissions and Draft's configuration.

## Access Draft

Use the Draft URL provided by your organization or administrator. Verify that it is the official deployment before signing in or authorizing a third-party account.
