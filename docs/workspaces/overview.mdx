# Workspaces and collaboration

This guide covers general practices for organizing work across X-ion products. Specific workspace, team, and sharing controls vary by product and deployment. Use only the controls available in your account.

## Organize work around outcomes

- Use clear names that describe the project, campaign, or goal.
- Keep related conversations, files, and decisions together where the product supports it.
- Record important decisions and unresolved questions.
- Separate drafts and experiments from approved or published work.
- Review access before sharing material outside your team.

## Share access deliberately

Before inviting someone or connecting a service, consider what information they may access and what actions they may perform. Grant only the permissions needed for the task. Remove access when it is no longer required, using the available account or provider controls.

## Keep work understandable

Use a consistent naming convention, include enough context for another person to understand an item, and document handoffs. Do not place passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or other secrets in shared documents or prompts.

## Feature availability

Do not assume a product supports shared workspaces, real-time editing, roles, or approval flows unless those controls are present and documented for your deployment.
