# Age verification

New users complete a **date-of-birth** step before full access to AI chat. This is enforced on the server, not only in the browser.

## What you enter

Day, month, and year. Scrutium rejects invalid dates and future dates, and calculates age correctly around birthdays.

## Outcomes

- **Eligible** — continue onboarding and use the product  
- **Not eligible** — access to restricted features is blocked with a clear explanation  

Minimum age is **configurable** by policy for your deployment — it is not hard-coded in client UI alone.

## Existing users

Accounts that predate the feature may see a modal after deploy until verification is completed.
