# Policies, terms, and documentation scope

This page helps readers understand the role of product documentation and locate the policies that apply to their use of X-ion products.

## Documentation is product guidance

Guides explain workflows, configuration, and recommended practices. They do not replace applicable terms, privacy notices, provider terms, or organizational requirements.

## Before using a connected service

Review the connected provider's terms, privacy practices, permissions, and data handling. A connection may make information available to both systems according to the permissions you grant and the providers' respective policies.

## Interpret feature descriptions carefully

- A documented workflow may require configuration or account permissions.
- Third-party availability can change independently of X-ion.
- Examples are illustrative unless explicitly identified as verified, runnable examples.
- Planned capabilities should be labeled as planned and must not be presented as generally available.

## Privacy and security

Do not share passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or sensitive personal information in public issues. Use the relevant product settings and provider controls to manage access and data.

[Read account privacy guidance](/docs/account/privacy)

## Keep documentation trustworthy

Report unclear instructions, outdated screenshots, broken links, and differences between the guide and the actual product. Include the page URL and steps to reproduce the issue, while removing sensitive data.
