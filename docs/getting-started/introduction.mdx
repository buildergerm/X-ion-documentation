# Welcome to Scrutium

**Scrutium** is a modern AI assistant for chat, research, and building — private by default, built by **X-ion Team**.

## What you can do

- **Chat** — Ask questions, brainstorm, write, and reason with streaming responses and a clear thinking UI.
- **Research** — Use live tools for web search, weather, places, crypto, FX, time, and Wikipedia.
- **Build** — Create small apps and pages in Build mode, organized under Projects.
- **Library** — Browse media, files, and published apps from one place.
- **Memory** — Let Scrutium remember durable facts about you across chats (you control this in Settings).

## Design principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|--------|
| Private by default | Your chats and preferences stay on your account; guests are limited. |
| Clear feedback | Thinking time, tools, and status are visible — not a black box. |
| Product-first UI | Flat, professional layout inspired by modern AI products. |
| Safety | Age verification and parental controls where required. |

## Who it’s for

- Individuals who want a fast, private AI workspace
- Teams and builders who organize work in Projects
- Developers who may self-host Scrutium
